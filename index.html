Bursaries

A bursary takes a percentage off a learner's bill: needs-based, staff children, academic or sports awards. 1 Choose the class and the child · 2 press Change bursary and type the percentage · 3 check the new bill and press Save. This term's bill changes straight away; earlier terms do not. Every change is kept in "Who did what".

Backup and restore

Everything is kept on this device only. 1 Press Back up now at least once a week · 2 keep the file somewhere safe (a flash disk, or email it to yourself) · 3 to move to a new computer, open the app there and press Restore from a backup. The file holds the children's records, the money and the staff accounts, so keep it private.

The school's records are kept in the school database and backed up there. Back up now downloads a copy for your own files.

Danger zone

Permanently erase every record on this device. Press Back up now first.

SMS to parents

Fee reminders, absence alerts, notices and parents' sign-in codes go out as SMS through your school's own Africa's Talking account. The school pays Africa's Talking directly. Register a sender ID (your school's short name) with them so parents see who is texting.

The key is checked with Africa's Talking, then stored encrypted. Nobody can read it back, not even the director.
